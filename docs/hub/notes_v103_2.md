CHOPPAH 1.3.2: a screen reader.

- **CHOPPAH reads its screen aloud** for players who can't see it. Plug the FM-1 into a computer, open the [FM-1 Screen Reader](https://charlesvestal.github.io/fm1-reader/) in Chrome or Edge and click Connect. You hear the page, its knobs and their values, every message, play and stop, held keys and pads, and warnings, all spelled out in words (CMPLX is "complexity", 3/16 is "three sixteenths"). It works with the page's own voice or with VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS and TalkBack. Thanks to João Durães (WoodJack) for asking.
- Built on the latest fm1-base (c8e0599).
- The demo's star field has 48 stars instead of 64, which makes room for the reader.

Saved settings and patterns from 1.3.1 load as they were.
