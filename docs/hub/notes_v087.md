The letter to the Firmware Hub, at this release, and the release notes filled in.

- The letter names this release and the package that goes with it, and carries only what the Hub's rules ask for. It can go out: CHOPPAH has been tested on a real FM-1, installed over the official V15 and over another firmware, and another firmware installs again afterwards.
- The releases from v0.77 on now carry their own notes instead of only an automatic changelog: the Hub and the community read them.

The firmware itself is unchanged: eight Amen-family breaks, sixteen slices each, a live generator, a four-slot FX chain and a DJ filter — and it installs on the newer units with the UC25HQ80 flash.
