CHOPPAH 1.5.5: MATHCORE, the break as an instrument.

- **EDIT is now MATHCORE.** Eleven ready scenes sit on the black keys: rollers, AMEN, a snare-heavy groove, triplet snare rolls, fills, a snare rush, a riser and a drop, from 1 to 4 bars. Tap a key and its scene comes in: a groove on the next bar, a fill on the next beat, then your groove again by itself. Hold a key and it plays while held. Stopped, a key starts at once.
- **Four macros on K1–K4.** DENSITY thins the beat out or fills it up, MATH turns straight beats into 5 / 7 / 11 / 13 polyrhythms, SPEED goes half or double time, CHAOS throws in random slices, ratchets and reverses. Nothing to program.
- **A wheel you can watch.** A ring per lane turns at its own length, and every macro shows on it as you turn: added hits sparkle, dropped ones fade, the rings re-divide, shake and spark. The pads row shows which scene plays, which comes next and which play once.
- **It re-cuts the break.** Every hit is a slice of the break on its own voice, each cutting the last, so nothing is laid over the loop. The hats and snares vary by themselves (velocity, ghost notes, flams, other hits of the break, a laid-back feel), more in a phrase's last bar.
- **The Slice Map is heard, not set.** When a break loads, CHOPPAH finds its kicks, snares, hats and the rest; the K S H P letters sit on the slice blocks on HOME.
- **SEQ's kick, snare and hat lanes now play the break's own kick, snare and hat** in the loop's place. The synthesised kick, snare and hat are gone, and with them the MIXER's KICK and PERC faders.
- SEL + a black key chains scenes into a song; LANE and SCENE are there to edit a scene if you want to.

Saved settings and patterns load as they were; the kick's and snare's own sound settings are no longer used.
