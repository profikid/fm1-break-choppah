Hi Sven,

Here is CHOPPAH, a firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1.

Firmware name: CHOPPAH
Maker: Laurens Profittlich

Repository: https://github.com/profikid/fm1-break-choppah
Release: v0.76
Package: choppah-0.76.fwsc
Identity: FM-1_86
Licence: MPL-2.0, the complete source is in the repository

What it does: it is in the fmhub.json in the repository root, as the hub prefers.

Tested on a real FM-1: yes. Installed from the official V15 and from another
firmware, and another firmware installs again afterwards. It keeps its own store
(the settings, the panel, the Bluetooth keys and its 16 patterns) and does not
read other firmwares' projects or samples. The USB port shows up as
"CHOPPAH FM-1".

One thing worth knowing, since you wrote about it on the submit page: the
firmware works on the units with the UC25HQ80 flash. It accepts both flash
parts (the Puya P25Q80 and the UC25HQ80), in the app and in the update loader,
so those units can install CHOPPAH and leave it again. The fix is also an open
pull request on fm1-base: https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-base/pull/35

The browser version, the manual and the picture are in the fmhub.json too, and
the card's text can be changed there at any time.
