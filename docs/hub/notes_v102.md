CHOPPAH 1.2: ARP is rebuilt: an effect on every black key.

- **The overlay board is gone.** ARP's sixteen overlay effects on the white keys (DIVE, RISE, CHOP …) are removed.
- **The white keys play slices on ARP**, as on HOME, so one hand chops while the other throws effects.
- **Each black key is an effect slot** of the FX library: FILTER, CRUNCH, DRIVE, PUNCH, FLANGER, RING MOD, TREMOLO, REVERB, DELAY, EQ, TAPE or CLIP, over the whole mix (the break, the drums and the FX chain) before the limiter. It is on while you hold the key, with a 5 ms fade in and out; a REVERB or DELAY key stops taking sound in when let go, so its tail rings out.
- **SEL + a black key holds it on**, also on the other pages (an amber line under its pad); SEL + the key again lets it go.
- **Set them up in the FX page's own view.** The key pressed last (or ALGORITHM) is shown with its picture live, its modulator and its level in and out: PRESETS picks its effect, K1–K3 its settings, K4 its mix, and ARP again steps its MORE and MOD parts, as on the FX page. The pads under the strip name each key's effect and light while it is on.
- **Four effects sound at once**; a fifth key takes over the oldest. When the FM-1 runs out of time, the overload guard turns the pad effects off before the FX chain's slots.
- **The starting set:** low-pass, high-pass and band-pass filters, CRUNCH, DRIVE, RING MOD, FLANGER, a 1/16 TREMOLO gate, TAPE wow, a 3/16 DELAY and a REVERB. SAVE keeps what you set.

Saved settings from 1.1 load as they were; the black keys start with the set above.
