Everything in the catalogues brought up to date, and the test on a real FM-1 done.

- The letter to the Firmware Hub is at this release and carries only what the Hub's rules ask for. It can go out now: CHOPPAH has been tested on a real FM-1, installed over the official V15 and over another firmware, and another firmware installs again afterwards.
- The catalogues that name a version were brought to this release: the emulator's own list, the fm1-editor card (its "Latest" line, its release link and the strip at the top), and the Mooring catalogue's entry (the signed one, which Gustavo Barron publishes himself).
- mutant.cloud follows the releases on its own.

The firmware itself is unchanged: eight Amen-family breaks, sixteen slices each, a live generator, a four-slot FX chain and a DJ filter — and it installs on the newer units with the UC25HQ80 flash.
