CHOPPAH 1.0: the battery is in the header, next to the level meter.

- **A battery indicator in the header.** On the slicer page the header now carries a small battery right next to the level meter: three bars filling from the foot, red at one bar or none, and a bolt instead of the bars while a USB host powers the FM-1. It redraws when the level changes, not every frame, so it costs nothing in audio.
- **What "USB" means in the icon.** The platform reports four as "a USB host powers the FM-1", not as "the battery is full" — so the icon shows a bolt there instead of pretending the battery is full.
- **Everything else is unchanged.** Eight Amen-family breaks, sixteen slices each, a live generator, a four-slot FX chain and a DJ filter; Bluetooth audio sends the whole mix to a speaker or headphones; it installs on the newer units with the UC25HQ80 flash.

This is the first 1.x release: from here on the version numbers are 1.1, 1.2, … The firmware itself is the same as 0.99 plus the battery in the header.
