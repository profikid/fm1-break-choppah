CHOPPAH 1.3: MIDI reaches the black keys.

- **MIDI notes 64–74 (E4–D5) press black keys 1–11**, on any channel, held for as long as the note. They act exactly like the panel's keys: on HOME they fire the performance pads (ROLL, REVERSE, FREEZE, TAPE STOP …), on ARP the effect pads, and SEL + a note holds an ARP pad on.
- Notes 48–63 (C3–D#4) still play slices 1–16 and 36 / 38 / 42–46 the drums, as before.
- A very short note still presses its pad for a frame, so pads can be stabbed from a sequencer.

Saved settings and patterns from 1.2 load as they were.
