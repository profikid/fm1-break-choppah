CHOPPAH 1.3: the full MIDI map, a MIDI channel, and MIDI out.

- **MIDI channel** — GLOBAL has a fourth part, **CHANNEL** (GLO four times). K1 picks OMNI (the default: listens to every channel, sends on 1) or channel 1–16. Notes, CC and program changes follow it.
- **MIDI out** — K2 NOTES OUT (the panel's keys as notes), K3 CC OUT (the knobs), K4 PC OUT (pattern and break changes). Each starts off. Nothing that comes in is echoed back, so a DAW can record CHOPPAH and play it back into it.
- **CC in** — CC 20–23 turn K1–K4 on the page you're on; CC 24–39 set the FX chain's four slots (SET 1–3 and MIX each).
- **Program changes** — 0–15 pick pattern 1–16, 16–31 break 1–16.
- **Break notes** — notes 24–35 (C1–B1) pick break 1–12.
- **Song position** — with CLOCK IN on, CONTINUE starts where the DAW's song position is instead of from the top.

The whole map is in the manual, under GLOBAL → MIDI map. Saved settings and patterns from 1.2 load as they were (OMNI, nothing out).
