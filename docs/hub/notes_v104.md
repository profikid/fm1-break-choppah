CHOPPAH 1.4: a break of your own.

- **Put one break of your own on the FM-1.** The site has a new page, [Your break](https://profikid.github.io/fm1-break-choppah/breaks/): load any drum loop (WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC…), pick its two bars, and hear it chopped into sixteen slices exactly as CHOPPAH will play it. Play it as a loop, click or type the slices, let Chop rearrange them, preview it at another tempo. Then connect the FM-1 by USB and send it. Chrome or Edge on a computer.
- **It's break 1, and the break CHOPPAH starts with** whenever there is one. The eight built-in breaks follow.
- Connected, the page reads the break on the FM-1 back, shows and plays it, and can delete it.
- The break is made the same way as the built-in ones: levelled to match them, at 22.05 kHz (a little lower for breaks under 99.4 BPM, so two bars fit), sixteen slices snapped to the hits.
- It stays through CHOPPAH updates and RESET ALL.

Saved settings and patterns from 1.3.2 load as they were.
