CHOPPAH 1.5: the split, two breaks in one loop.

- **A bar of two breaks.** GLOBAL's third part is SPLIT: bar 1 plays a bar of break A, bar 2 a bar of break B, repitched to A's bar the way an old sampler would, so the two stay in time whatever their own tempos. K2 picks break B, K3 and K4 which of each break's two bars plays.
- **White keys 1–8 are A's slices, 9–16 B's.** A key latches its half from the next bar line, so the loop goes round bar 1 or bar 2 alone. Switch halves on the fly and the change lands in time. SEL + a key plays A then B again.
- **Swap the break under the loop.** With bar 2 latched, ALGORITHM turns break B, otherwise break A. The new break comes in at the next bar and the latch stays.
- **Layer a key of each.** Hold a key of each half and the second loops on voices of its own over the first.
- **The header, redone.** It shows both breaks of a split ("AMEN + THINK"), with the battery at the right edge, then the level meter and the tempo, all on one line.
- **Bluetooth audio out is gone**, to make room for the split. Bluetooth MIDI stays.
