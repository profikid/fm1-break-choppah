CHOPPAH 1.6: a pack of your own breaks, up to sixteen.

- **Up to sixteen breaks of your own.** The site's Your breaks page makes a pack: add breaks one by one from your own samples, or a whole page of Cyberworm's Amens from the library at once. The pack can be renamed, reordered, heard and sent in one go.
- **344 KB for your breaks,** in two banks of the flash. That is about ten breaks at 170 BPM at the built-in breaks' quality. Pick 16 kHz for a third more, or 11.025 kHz for all sixteen. The page shows how full each bank is, and says by how much a pack is too big.
- **Your breaks come first.** On the FM-1, ALGORITHM gives your pack first, in its order, then the eight built in. The pack's first break is the one CHOPPAH starts with.
- **Read back, kept, shared.** Connected, the page lists the breaks on the FM-1, and Bring them here puts them back in the pack to change and send again. The page keeps the pack in your browser, and Export / Import save and load it as one .chb file.
- **A 1.4 or 1.5 break stays.** A break of your own sent before 1.6 comes up as a pack of one. With the new page, a CHOPPAH 1.4 or 1.5 gets the pack's first break.

Saved settings, patterns and scenes load as they were. Sending a pack sets the split's break B back to "the break after A".
