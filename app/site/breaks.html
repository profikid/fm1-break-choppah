Your break

Put one break of your own on the FM-1, beside the eight built in. Load a sample, hear it chopped into sixteen slices exactly as CHOPPAH will play it, and send it over USB. While it is on the FM-1, it is break 1 and the one CHOPPAH starts with. Chrome or Edge, on a computer.

1. The FM-1

Plug the FM-1 in by USB with CHOPPAH 1.4 or later running, then connect.

Not connected.

2. A sample

A drum loop of whole bars: WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC or anything else your browser plays. Two bars are used; a one-bar loop is played twice.

Drop a sample here, or click to choose one

Placing the slices: drag a slice's start (the amber lines) on the waveform; the close-up below shows the one you picked, 60 ms either side, where a click or a drag places it exactly. ← and → move it 1 ms (Shift: 10 ms), ↑ and ↓ pick the next or the previous one, Esc lets go. Each move plays the slice. The first slice starts at the loop's start and the last ends at its end. Changing the bars, tempo, slices or level starts the slices again; Reset slices does too.

Click a slice, or press 1–8 and Q–I, to hear it alone. Chop plays the slices in a new order every bar, the way the generator rearranges a break. The preview repitches with the tempo, as CHOPPAH's SPEED mode.

3. Send it

CHOPPAH must be stopped: writing the flash pauses the sound. It replaces the break already there.
